# Monster Stacks (retired)

This experiment graduated. Canonical procedure: [[Burners Referee Guide#Monsters at speed (preferred Referee mode)]]. Worked paint notes below are kept for reference only.

**Monsters have Hit Dice.** Deal each monster a **Monster Stack** of **2 × HD** playing cards. **Face-down = closed** (armored / shell). **Face-up = open** (meat). The stack is a life clock — pop closed first; when faces show, **Cracked!** Ignore rank and suit. Heat is a separate dish for the whole enemy side (scene gauge — it does **not** refresh each round).

## Why

Session 3 pain: notepad HP, a shared Heat/HD mishmash, and weak “does it look hurt?” progress. Players dump Fuel and want to see it matter; the Referee wants low bookkeeping and a public track.

**AC 1–2 as a flat soak was a Referee math tax** — players never changed tactics for it. Soft kit does not invent a soak number; it **paints more face-down cards into the 2 × HD stack**. Naked / sushi / “kinda tough hide” stay **all face-up**. Tiny book AC does not invent a soak.

## One rule

1. Player announces **damage** (Fuel faces — “fifteen!”). Keep the big number.
2. Ref: **Hits = ⌈damage ÷ 5⌉** (always round **up**).
3. **Closed?** (any face-down remains, or flesh that shrugs your edge) → **−1 Hit**. That is the whole state: armor is one card of soak — **−5 damage** in effect. No spend waives it; Crack the shell or bring the edge the flesh fears.
4. **Right tool** — claim it in fiction (*“I slice the sushi!”* / *“I crack these crabs with my poleax!”*): burn **1 more Fuel** than the blow otherwise allows; its face **adds to the damage**. The claim buys a deeper blow, not a waiver — fiction stays in the player’s hands.
5. Apply Hits — **face-down first, then face-up**. **0** → bounce. Pop cards onto the table.

| Damage | Hits (ceil) | Closed (−1) | Open |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1–5 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| 6–10 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 11–15 | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| 16–20 | 4 | 3 | 4 |

| Piece | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Hits** | ⌈damage ÷ 5⌉ always. Each Hit pops one card. |
| **Hit Dice** | The stack: **2 × HD** cards (face-down + face-up). |
| **Face-down** | Closed — armored / shell. Peel first. Soft kit **adds face-downs into the mix**; hard shell → mostly/all face-down; no kit → all face-up. |
| **Face-up** | Open — meat. Pluck after the last face-down is gone (or from the start if sushi). |
| **Right tool** | Player names the fiction and burns **one extra Fuel** into the blow. Suggest poleax vs shell, slicing vs sushi, silver, holy — but the claim is theirs. |
| **Cracked!** | Last face-down gone — faces show; the armor −1 ends (flesh that shrugs your edge still shrugs it). |
| **Heat** | One shared dish — not the Hit Dice stack. Rubric: aim Heat at shown **HD**; lower if disadvantaged, higher if it owns the room. Scene gauge — does not refresh each round. |
| **Dead** | Stack empty. Scoop. |

### Paint (mix inside 2 × HD)

| Fiction | Mix |
| --- | --- |
| Naked / sushi / tough hide, no kit | **All face-up** |
| Soft kit (leather, shield, light gambeson) | **Some face-down** (orc HD 1 → **1 down / 1 up**) |
| Mail / heavier kit | **More face-down** |
| True shell / plate / scales | **Mostly or all face-down** (crab HD 3 → **6 face-down**) |

Do not add a separate armor track outside the 2 × HD budget. Soft armor is “more face-down,” not “+1 soak forever.”

**Tactics:** big damage while closed; scraps after Cracked. Claim the right tool for the extra die when it matters. Players shout damage; the Ref converts.

**Fuel economy:** a **1** and a **2** stacked are 3 → **1 Hit** (ceil); closed → bounce. Keep scraps for Defend or to ride a **3+** toward 5+; the right tool's extra die is how a stuck blow punches through.

## Kit

**Playing cards — settled.** A cheap deck; each monster gets its own **Monster Stack**. Pre-build a stack per monster (crab: six face-down; Devil sushi: sixteen face-up; orc in leather: one down on one up). As Hits land, **pop cards off onto the table**. When the last face-down is gone, faces show → **Cracked!** Ignore rank and suit.

- **Heat:** one shared dish (dice). Do not raid it for Hit Dice. Heat ≈ shown HD, nudge for who’s in control.
- Speech: players say **damage** (and optional right-tool claim + Fuel); Ref converts and pops cards.

An older Wåndyr-2 note (*Burners Monster Dice*, vaulted obsolete): **the table shows the fight**; **don’t overload one token**; **pluck the dead**. Leave the POW-tier tables in the vault.

## Multi-foe

Five orcs, an ogre, a warlock, three dire wolves is runnable. Budget is **2 × HD cards per body**: orcs HD 1 × 5 = **10**; wolves HD 3–4 × 3 ≈ **18–24**; ogre ≈ **8–10**; warlock ≈ **4–6** → ~40–50 cards. Soft kit only changes the **face mix**, not the count.

## Worked example — crabs (HD 3 → 6 face-down)

Closed while any face-down remains (−1) — no waiver. The poleax claim buys an extra die, not a pass.

| Blow | Hits | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Herrick **15** | ⌈15÷5⌉=3, −1 → **2 face-down** | **4 face-down** |
| Reinhardt **10** + extra die (face 4) via poleax claim → dmg **14** | ⌈14÷5⌉=3, −1 → **2 face-down** | **2 face-down** |
| Anyone **6** | ⌈6÷5⌉=2, −1 → **1 face-down** | **1 face-down** |
| Anyone **6** | ⌈6÷5⌉=2, −1 → **1 face-down** → **Cracked!** | **0** — all-face-down crab **dead** |

## Worked example — Wet Devil (HD 8 → 16 face-up)

Sushi — all face-up, but the flesh **shrugs everything except the slice**: closed (−1) to bludgeon and point; a slicing edge is open. Claiming the slice also burns the extra die.

**Table kit:** sixteen face-up under the mini. Public stack.

Sample dump **5, 6, 11, 5, 10, 9, 2, 3, 2** — all wrong-edge blows:

| 6 | 2 | 1 | 15 |

**5 cards off** if nobody slices — she is barely hurt. When Herrick shouts *“I slice!”*, his edge is open (full ceil, no −1) and the claim lets him burn an extra die into the blow.

## Retired

- Resistance **5** ladder (and Resistance 1–4)
- Flat AC soak (especially AC 1–2 math tax)
- Separate armor-die band *outside* 2 × HD
- Floor/ceil by state (replaced by always-ceil + closed −1)
- Right tool as a paid waiver (1 Fuel to skip the closed −1) — the −1 always stands now; the claim buys one extra Fuel burned into the blow
- Free soft damage while face-down remains
- White-costs-2 chip pricing; poker chips / dice-as-tokens as the preferred kit
- **Cracked!** = half HP + Spark, then no Heat refresh
- Shared Heat/HD mishmash as the life bar
- Notepad HP as the preferred table track
- Referee-only right tool (weapon auto-opens) — players claim it with Fuel + fiction
