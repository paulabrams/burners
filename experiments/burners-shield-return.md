# Shield Return, Cover Die

A shield keeps you in the fight. A wall takes the arrow for you. Neither one
makes a big smash smaller. #needs-playtest

---

## The rules

**Cover.** Cover does not add dice. When you Defend from Cover, one non-6 Fuel
die you spend refills: it cuts the blow at its spent face, then you roll a fresh
die into your pool. One refill per Defend. Full cover and you cannot be targeted.

**Shield.** A shield is Cover you carry. For a shield refill, the spent die must
show the shield's slots: buckler 2, heater 3, rotella / kite 4, tower 5. An
**enchanted shield** may refill on its slots or a 6.

*Example: you Defend behind a heater and burn a 3. The 3 cuts the blow, then the
shield refills one die into your pool.*

- Using a creature as Cover: a 6 cannot refill, but it is **Hit the Cover**. The
  shot hits them instead of you, as if it had been aimed at them from the start,
  and they may not declare Cover to put it back on you.

**Burning Fuel.** "No reroll" gains one exception: Cover refills. In the dirt you
still re-roll everything you burn; Cover adds nothing there.

---

## Why

- Big hits get through. A pressed club still needs its full weight in dice; the
  board only keeps you Defending longer. Shields are for many small blows —
  arrows, spears, a mob.
- Size matters on the table. Every shield returns a die as often (one face in
  six), but a bigger board returns a bigger face, and costs more slots.
- Cover does not drain Fuel. Behind a wall, the die you spend can refill after it
  stops the arrow.
- Against the Fallen, a refilled die still counts toward cutting the whole blow,
  so a shield-bearer can stay untouched and still hold Fuel for the fight
  (see [[Burners Burn Undead#Touch of the Fallen]]).

## Rejected along the way

- Shield mulligan: re-roll any Defend Fuel with a shield. Fiddly, and it still
  costs Fuel behind a wall.
- Cover as a flat cut by shield size. Stacks with armor into immunity to small
  blows.
- Free cover die on shields. Takes about 3.5 off everything, clubs included.
- Exact match to the attack returns the die. Cannot match a pressed 7–10, and a
  held face loops against fixed quotes.
- Wall spend-and-return. Still costs Fuel, and needs a Spark exception.
- Returned die keeps its face. Infinite block.
- Returned die waits for the next Roll Initiative. Too weak against a mob.
- Dropping shield dice from Initiative. More edits than it is worth; the return
  stands on its own.
