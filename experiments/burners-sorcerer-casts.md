# Sorcerer Casts, Double Down, Targets

Three linked Sorcerer mods. The mage stops waiting on a 6 to do anything interesting:
Spark stays an opening in the steel; a working's width is something you buy.

---

## The rules

### 1. Casts per Action — undecided

**Rejected:** one extra spell per Sorcerie level (the Veteran-Attacks mirror). Three
candidates remain:

- **A. One spell per Cast action** — the rule as it stands today. Double Down and
  Targets below still work; the double is paid in Fuel alone.
- **B. Spell levels totalling Sorcerie + 1** — one Cast action may hold several
  workings whose levels sum to at most **Sorcerie + 1**. At Sorcerie 1 that is two
  1sts; at Sorcerie 3, a 3rd and a 1st, or four 1sts.
- **C. Spell levels totalling Sorcerie, period** — the sum is at most **Sorcerie**.
  At Sorcerie 1, one 1st; at Sorcerie 3, a 3rd, a 2nd and a 1st, or three 1sts. Your
  biggest known spell always fills the whole Action. Watch the interaction: if a
  doubled-down working counts one level higher, a Sorcerie 1 mage can never bend —
  under C the Double Down price must be Fuel only, or apprentices are locked out.

Under a level budget (B or C):

- Each working still burns at least its level (SL) in Fuel dice — and still **taps its
  card from your hand**. The hand is the real limitation: Fuel refills every round, but
  a multi-cast round taps cards you will not have ready again without burns or sleep.
- Recasts and copies from Burning count their levels against the budget.
- A doubled-down working counts **one level higher** — under B, at Sorcerie 1 a bent
  1st (counts as 2) fills the whole budget; under C this must not count against the
  budget at all (Fuel-only price), or Sorcerie 1 cannot bend.
- **Canting:** Journeymen stay at one working per Cast unless ruled otherwise.

### 2. Double Down

A spell's text is its scope — until you double down. Pay the surcharge in Fuel: the
working may now **bend the rules of the spell**, doing something that is not written
there.

The bend stays inside the spell's own idea. It is the same card at the same level —
never the next spell up the list, never a working of a level higher. Once per working.

**Price — undecided.** Doubling SL is rejected: too cheap at low levels, much too
expensive at high (a doubled 6th burns 12 dice and fumbles 62% of the time — two or
more 1s among the dice burned). Flat surcharge candidates:

| SL | plain cast (fumble) | SL + 1 (fumble) | SL + 2 (fumble) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 die (17% — one-die clause) | 2 dice (2.8%) | 3 dice (7.4%) |
| 3 | 3 dice (7.4%) | 4 dice (13%) | 5 dice (20%) |
| 6 | 6 dice (26%) | 7 dice (33%) | 8 dice (40%) |

- **SL + 1** — one clean number (the original "+1 level" instinct); fixes the high
  end, leaves a bent 1st at 2 Fuel.
- **SL + 2** (lean) — fixes both ends: a bent 1st costs 3 Fuel, near half an
  apprentice's opening pool; a bent 6th is 8 dice, expensive but live.

The fumble table already scales the risk with spell level, so the price does not have
to do all the work — doubling was double-counting the danger.

- The common bend is **targeting**: a spell that affects no one directly (a placed
  web, an ice sheet, a fog) may now affect **one creature** — the first target rides
  the double. See *Targets* below.
- A spell that already targets simply comes out **wider** — more of what it already
  does.

Notes:

- Replaces Spark-to-broaden. A Spark never bends a rule — bending is bought. Instead,
  **Spark: Cast again** (the Flurry of spells): when a cast Sparks, resolve the working
  (the 6 counts on the total), then Fuel the Spark with a full new cast — at least SL
  Fuel dice, tapping another ready card. The Spark-cast rides outside any Cast budget,
  as a Counterattack rides outside the attack allotment. Unfueled, it fades.
- The level never changes: Ward Pact still sees a 1st-level working, dispel-by-level
  unchanged, fumbles read the dice as ever (more dice = more 1s = the price of ambition).
- Sibling of **Copy** in Burning: Copy pays a card for a second working; Double Down
  pays extra Fuel for one wider working.
- A doubled-down working is the caster's whole Cast action (under budget B it counts
  one level higher; at Sorcerie 1 that fills the budget) — the apprentice going for
  the pin is all-in.
- **Term:** *double down* — gambling register matches the game's voice ("what will you
  burn?") and the name carries the price. Runner-up: *stoke* (fire family with Burn /
  Fuel / Spark, but hides the cost). Rejected: *mirror* (collides with Copy and with
  Illusione), *twin* (D&D metamagic baggage, implies exactly two targets).

### 3. Targets: unnamed is none

If a spell does not name a creature — or a count of them — it is **placed in the
world and catches no one**. To affect a creature directly you must **Double Down**;
that buys the first target. **Each additional creature is one extra Fuel die**; those
dice land in the **cast total**. The Referee may grant **free Defend dice** when the
target is a poor match for the working (an ogre against a web; bronze in the water —
same move as Man-handle's free dice).

- "Names a creature" includes *a creature*, *the target*, *Sorcerie creatures* — not
  only a numeral. Command, Healing Touch, Wizard Light on eyes are unaffected.
- Web Weave as written does not pin a sword-arm — that is a Double Down, and at
  Sorcerie 1 it is the caster's whole Action.
- Extra-target dice ride the same cast total — and raise the fumble odds. Targets
  Defend as ever.

**The motivating table case** (Lothian, the *No God* bridge fight): Bellemy's Web
Weave chained **four skeletons** together with a webby rope — fun and smart, but the
price was one Spark plus one Fuel (the maximum Spark-broaden even allowed). Much too
cheap: this rule codifies that gap. Repriced: Double Down (2–3 Fuel at SL 1) buys the
bend and the first skeleton; three more are +3 dice — **5–6 Fuel and the whole Cast
action** to take four bodies out of a fight. Bone men against sticky web is a fair
match, so no free Defend dice; the Referee prices resilience, not cleverness.

### How they spend together

Web Weave doubled down pins one sword-arm — 2 or 3 Fuel by the price chosen; a second
body is +1 die; the caster's whole Action. Under budget B a Sorcerie 1 mage could
instead cast two plain 1sts; under A the choice is which single working the round gets.

## Open questions

- **Casts per Action: A (single spell), B (levels totalling Sorcerie + 1), or C
  (levels totalling Sorcerie)?** The Veteran mirror is rejected; this is the headline
  decision. Under C, Double Down must price in Fuel only or Sorcerie 1 cannot bend.
- **Double Down price: SL + 1 or SL + 2?** Doubling SL is rejected (see the fumble
  table); lean SL + 2.
- ~~Does a caster get any Spark option at all now?~~ **Resolved: Spark: Cast again**
  — a Spark on a cast may Fuel a full second casting (new card, ≥ SL dice), outside
  any Cast budget. A Spark never bends a rule; bending stays bought (Double Down).
- Should Canting Journeymen ever multi-cast (Craft mirroring Sorcerie)?
