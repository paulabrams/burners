# What Does Pushing a Spell Cost?

## The problem

A Spark on a cast lets you push a spell past what it normally does. The rules say you pay extra Fuel for it, but not how much buys what:

> **When a cast Sparks.** Fuel it with one or more additional Fuel dice, up to SL, and burn that Fuel on the *working itself*: another target caught, a beat longer, a ward pierced (Referee's call). Add no Fuel and the Spark fades.

Is one more target one Fuel? Is a longer web the same price as a second skeleton? The Referee ends up making it up each time.

## Proposed wording

> **When a cast Sparks.** Fuel the Spark to push the spell past its text: another target caught, a beat longer, a ward pierced. One Spark can buy as many pushes as you can Fuel, one Fuel each. This Fuel buys the push; it doesn't add to the cast total or the damage. Add no Fuel and the Spark fades.

Two changes from the rule as it stands: no "up to SL" cap, and the Fuel is spent on the push, not on the spell's total.

## Options for the price

- **One Fuel per push.** Each Fuel buys one thing: one more target, one more round, one ward pierced. (Lean.) It answers "how much?" at the table without math.
- **The spell's level per push.** Each push costs the spell's level in Fuel. It scales: cheap for a 1st, dear for a 5th.
- **Referee names it.** What the rule does now. Flexible, but it's the problem.

## The Lothian web

At the *No God* bridge, Bellemy's Web Weave chained four skeletons for one Spark and one Fuel. At one Fuel per push, the three extra skeletons cost three Fuel: a Spark and three dice, most of a level 1 Sorcerer's round.

## Set aside: Double Down

Burn double the spell's level and push the spell with no Spark needed. Simple and it scales, but it's a new mechanic for Sorcerers only. If we ever do it, it should be a whole-game rule (steel, Wards, Approach rolls), not a magic patch.

## Loose end

The update makes your Arcana's slots add to every cast. The rules' *Your 6s* line ("take your gear instead of the Spark: add the Arcana's slots") then gives nothing. Cut that line when the update goes into the rules, so a 6 on a cast is a Spark like any other.
