# Turn Tracker

A world Turn is ten minutes. Track Turns on paper where the players can see it. Write a check mark `x` on one line per Turn. Write "noise" or `!` after the check if they made noise that might alert monsters; write "extra noise" or `!!` as appropriate. Shouting, kicking in doors, fighting, casting spells, and the like all count as noise.

Optionally record a brief note about what happened, light sources, wandering monster checks, etc.

Example:

```
X  enter, spike the door
X  Bellamy lantern (6) · WM 5
X ! forced the grate
X  WM 2
X  search the shrine
X  WM 4
X !! fight in the nave · lantern out · WM 3
X  WM 2
```

## Wandering Monsters (WM)

Check for wandering monsters at the start of every other Turn, or immediately when the characters make noise (i.e. when you write the noise `!`). Roll 1d6 and write the result ("WM n") on the line so you know you made the check.

- Quiet: 1-in-6
- Noise: 2-in-6 if there was noise (`!`) in the past 20 minutes or extra noise (`!!`) in the past hour.
- Dark: 2-in-6 if quiet, 3-in-6 with noise and/or wounds.

## Light Sources

- Torch: 30 feet for 1 hour (6 Turns). Bundle of 6: 1 gp, 1 slot.
- Lantern: 30 feet for 4 hours (24 Turns) per flask. Lantern 10 gp, 1 slot; oil 2 gp, 1 slot.
- Candle: 5 feet for 1 hour (6 Turns). Bundle of 6: 1 gp, 1 slot.
- Rag-on-a-stick: as candle, but only up to 20 minutes if you are lucky. Costs nothing, 1 slot.
