# Burners October 13th Updates

**1. Holding.** Hold your move, your Action, some of your attacks, or any mix of
them.

- The rank behind holds its move and steps up when the front foe falls.
- A fighter in a doorway holds his attack and strikes the first one that steps
  into reach.
- An archer holds her shot until her friend is out of the line.
- A Veteran with a longsword uses one attack now and holds the other: "My
  weapon's longer. As he steps up, I stick him."

However, don't expect to always go first. In the longsword example, a spearman foe has a longer reach and will attack first. Compare using item slots.

**2. Fuel.** Your maximum is your level + empty slots (up to 3). Roll your
maximum in round 1. At each Roll Initiative after that, drop any dice you don't
want, then roll new dice until you're back at your maximum. Weapons, shields and
Arcana give no dice. Sorcerie slots don't count as empty.

That's 4 dice at level 1, 5 at level 2, 6 at level 3. With 4 dice you can make
one big two-handed blow and Defend twice, or two big blows and no Defend, or one
attack and Defend three times.

Retainers: half their level, round down, plus empty slots (up to 3), with a
minimum of 1 die. A level 1 retainer rolls its empty slots, one die below a
Burner; in full kit it still gets 1.

Leave your pack in a room that feels safe. Caught with it on, you roll only your
level in dice. Dropping it mid-fight takes your Action.

**3. Traits matter.** When you burn a 1 using an item you have the Trait for,
add the item's slots to it. A weapon on a blow, a shield on a Defend, an Arcana
on a cast. A dagger makes it a 2, a heater a 4, a tower a 6 (not a Spark).

Each item upgrades one 1 per blow or Defend. Burn three 1s on a Defend with sword
and buckler: the buckler makes one a 3, the sword makes one a 2, and the third
stays a 1. Axe and buckler: 3, 1, 1. A halberd blow with two 1s: 6 and 1.

A weapon with a **Handguard** parries: on a Defend it counts as 1 slot, or 2
for rings, a swept hilt or a basket (`Handguard2`). A weapon with no guard
(messer, axe, mace, spear) adds nothing on a Defend.

| On a Defend | A 1 becomes |
| --- | ---: |
| No guard | 1 |
| Cross hilt, finger buckler | 2 |
| Rapier, basket hilt, buckler | 3 |
| Heater, round shield | 4 |
| Rotella, kite | 5 |
| Tower | 6 |

**4. Classic side initiative.** Roll 1d6 per side to see who goes first. (If a
side is split up in the fight, the Referee may give each part its own d6.) A
surprised side goes last in round 1. Light kit is quick: add 1 to your side's
roll for you alone for every 4 empty slots.

**5. Fatigued.** Hard going wears you out. While Fatigued, your Fuel maximum is
halved (round up). An Hour Rest clears it. It doesn't stack.

- A fight that runs 10 rounds (a full Turn) leaves everyone in it Fatigued.
- Six days of travel without a day's rest.
- Anything else that would wear you out, on the Referee's call: you come out of
  the rapids half-drowned, run a long chase, climb all afternoon, go a night
  without sleep.

A Journeyman keeps the group going longer. Add his Craft to the hours or days
before the group he's guiding becomes Fatigued.

Fatigue costs no HP and no Heat. HP is harm, Heat is what you owe, Fatigued is
wear.

**6. Roll monster damage.** The Referee always rolls monster damage, using the
monster's damage die as printed. When an attack die rolls its top face (6 on a
d6, 8 on a d8), roll one more of that die and add it. Only once; the extra die
doesn't roll again. If several dice roll their top face, it's the Referee's
call: when the attack is meant to be deadly, each one adds its own die.

The roll is the most the blow can do. The Referee may always deal less when the
situation calls for it. It's the same blow, not a second attack: one Defend, one touch, one dose
of poison, one Trait Curse.

**7. Defend.** 2 dice, or 3 with a raised shield. If nothing in your hands can
turn the blow (bare hands against a blade, a bow against a spear, an arrow with
no shield or Cover), Defend 1. Anything you could parry with counts: a dagger, a
staff, a torch. With *wrestling* or *boxing*, bare hands can turn fists and
grabs.

## Rules text that changes with them

- "You cannot hold an Action or an attack until a trigger" goes. Change 1
  replaces it.
- The round 2 refill (one die per level) is replaced by the top-off. For
  retainers, "Fuel level dice / refill: ⌊retainer level / 2⌋, minimum 1" in
  Muster's Half Power table and the FAQ becomes half level plus empty slots, with
  the minimum of 1 on the total, not the level part. Manage still rounds the
  level part up.
- "A die is used at its face and discarded. No reroll." stays. Its one
  exception: a 1 on a trained item adds the item's slots.
- In Arms and Armor, **Defensive** goes and **Handguard** takes its place. The
  old Handguard (AC 2 for the weapon hand) goes with it. Weapons tagged
  Defensive and Handguard today become `Handguard2` (parrying dagger, cutlass,
  sidesword, rapier, Swiss saber, greatsword). Defensive alone becomes
  `Handguard` (arming sword, short sword, sabre, falchion, longsword, the Alu
  swords, Vakaar falchion). Polearms and the shield punch lose it.
- The finger buckler is too small for Defend 3.
- Sorcerie's "Cast fumble (combat)" goes. Casts don't fumble in combat, so a 1
  burned into a cast is only a low die, or the Arcana's slots plus one.
- Out of combat, a cast's natural 2 fumbles only if the working is ambitious
  (pushed past what the card plainly does) or you cast without paying a ready
  card (a scroll read cold, a book, a working that isn't in your hand).
  Otherwise it doesn't fumble.
- Winded, or Fuel you're forced to lose with an empty pool: you top off one die
  short next round.
- Counting 3s and 4s for Initiative, and each foe group rolling its own d6, go.
  Change 4 replaces them.
- Wear stops being paid in Heat. The Referee Guide's Grind section (a long crawl
  raises Heat) and the Journeyman's cold-rain example (6 Heat, cut by Craft)
  become Fatigued, with Craft stretching how long the group lasts.
- Flat monster damage quotes go: the quote table in the Referee Guide's
  *Monsters at speed* and the damage-die-to-quote table in OSE Conversions.
  Change 6 replaces them.
- "Melee one die, or no limit if you made a melee attack this round" goes, and
  so does "the archer or mage caught in reach… cuts each melee blow with a single
  die." Change 7 replaces them. Missiles stay 1 die, 2 with Cover. Examples that
  lean on Defending without limit need redoing: the Aldric ogre and
  dirty-fighting examples, Brand, Sefa and Kragg in the examples of play, and the
  mobs line in the Referee Guide.

## Watch for in play

- Light kit. Fuel stops at 3 empty slots, so slots past that only buy speed. A
  Burner goes first 42% of the time, 58% with 4 empty slots (+1), 72% with 8
  (+2). It only matters when the party ties or loses by a little.
- Fatigue. Fights should end in 2–4 rounds, so 10 rounds only catches a grind.
  Undead and constructs don't tire. A Fatigued Sorcerer may be short of dice for
  his biggest spells.
- Defend. Whether you swung no longer matters, only what's in your hands, so a
  fighter holding his attack in a doorway still Defends 2. Shields give no Fuel
  now; Defend 3 and their 1s are their job.
- 1s are worth keeping. With a tower or a polearm, a 1 beats any reroll, so it
  stays through the top-off. With a dagger it's a 2, and you drop it.
- Halberd against sword and board, same 5 slots. The halberd's 1 is a 6 on a
  blow and a 1 on a Defend; the sword and heater's is a 3 on a blow and a 4 on
  a Defend, with Defend 3.
- Greatsword against sword and buckler. The greatsword turns a 1 into a 5 on the
  attack and still parries at 3; two 1s on a Defend are 3 and 1. Sword and
  buckler's two 1s are 3 and 2, with Defend 3. The two-hander hits, the shield
  man turns blows.
