# XP and Advancement

Now in [[Burners Experience]], *Gaining Traits* in [[Burners Adventure Game]], and
[[Burners Burn Undead]].

## The changes

**1. The deed pays, not the finding.** Treasure XP comes from wresting it from
the dungeon: a monster, a trap, a lock, the tide. It's credited at the deed, to
those there.

- Kill a guardian beside its hoard: its XP plus the hoard, found or not.
- Kill it away from its hoard: its XP only.
- Get past a trap or lock into the room it guards: that room's treasure, taken or
  not. The trap itself pays nothing.
- Sneak past or go around: only what you carry off.

**2. Town pays coin, never XP.** Robbing folk, looting a camp you scared off,
killing townsfolk or their animals: no XP. Bandits and pirates who come at you
in the wild are foes like any monster. Undefended treasure pays gold, not XP.

**3. Staking a claim.** Enter a lair while its guardian lives and come out alive,
and that hoard is yours to earn when you go back and take it, however the
guardian died. Nobody else earns it.

**4. Credited once.** Each gold piece is credited once. Finding loot that's
already been credited pays gold only.

**5. Their own reward.** Magic items, weapons, armor, tools, monster parts,
bounties, keeps and ships pay no XP. Some sell; that's purse money.

**6. Monsters pay what they're worth.** A foe with nothing to guard still pays
for how dangerous it was.

**7. Hidden XP.** The Referee keeps the totals and tells you when you level. Ask
how close you are and you get "about halfway" or "close," not a number.

**8. Traits on waking.** When you wake from sleep, take the Traits you earned at
the table: none, one, or several, up to the four for your next level. Each one
points to something you did there. Wake from a safe sleep with the four Traits
and enough XP, and you level up.

*Oct 14 (October 13 update 8): Traits and levels come when you burn something,
not when you wake. It counts if you gave up something you won't get back; the
Referee judges. Paul: "It's not sleeping. It's burning." And: "There's no dream
sequence when you sleep. It's when you burn something." It plays to the game's
name, any burn will do, and leveling becomes a story beat. Open: whether the
four Traits stay a requirement.*

**9. Trait Curse.** The undead don't drain levels or Traits. They curse a random
Trait. It still works, but its 1s hate you.

## Why

**The problem.** Claim-based XP (1 XP per gp when treasure is taken) had three
faults at the Lothian table:

- Players would rather fight than loot, which suits Burners, but the claim rule
  paid the loot far more than the fights.
- It paid whoever was standing there. The 40 gp under the Drowned Lord's coffin
  went to six characters in Session 6, two of whom joined long after he died.
  The torc beside it was left behind and washed away, so the three who killed
  him got nothing for it.
- "Do we have enough XP?" every session. Advancement turned into scorekeeping.

Saxon put it plainly: "by the numbers so far, stealing is way more profitable
from an XP perspective," and "we don't seem to get treasure when I'm here."

**Why the deed.** It pays the risk, not the luck of being in the room. It also
gives the Referee an honest lever: raise unfound treasure and the players earn
XP for a deed they already did, with the gold still ahead of them.

**Why the lair.** You have to be beside the hoard: the same chamber, the coffin
it rose from. The same dungeon isn't enough. A gatekeeper guards the door, not
the rooms behind it. A chimera killed a hundred miles from home doesn't hand you
its lair.

**Why traps pay nothing themselves.** Beating a trap counts for the room it
guards. If traps paid on their own, players would hunt traps instead of
treasure.

**Why the claim is a plain fact.** Were you in the lair while the guardian
lived? That's the whole test. Nobody argues over whose plan lured it out. In
*The Hobbit*, Bard kills Smaug over Lake-town and gets the dragon's XP only. The
dwarves went into the Mountain with Smaug alive, so the hoard is theirs to earn.
A stranger who finds it later gets the gold.

**Why parts ride on the kill.** If parts paid XP, the deed rule would pay them at
the kill whether anyone harvested them or not, so they may as well be part of
the kill. Lothian's vampire dust was first paid as 300 coin XP and moved onto
the draugr, who now pays 335 instead of 35.

**Why hidden XP.** It ends the scorekeeping, and cumulative XP means nothing is
lost by not knowing the number. It only works if "not yet" doesn't drag on, so
give a rough distance.

**Tried and dropped (Oct 8).**

- Trait slots: one per session attended, up to four pending, filled later from
  a remembered moment. Replaced by Traits on waking. No quota, nothing banked.
- One moment, one Trait: dropped. A moment that cut deep can earn more than one.
- Players asking "do I have enough?" once they hold four Traits: replaced by the
  Referee telling them.
- Trait Drain removed a Trait; it now curses one instead.

## Lothian worked example (through Session 8)

Old awards stand; nothing is clawed back. New credits are guarded treasure the
party never took.

- **Platinum torc, 100 gp.** In the Drowned Lord's coffin. Reinhardt, Herrick and
  Bellemy killed him beside it in Session 0: 33 each.
- **Grey Hart cargo, 6,250 gp.** Credited at the Wet Devil kill in Session 3; she
  sorted the ship's loot in her own lair. Split 7 ways (five Burners plus Ylva and
  Gar as retainers): 892 each, retainers 446. The Referee raised this after
  Session 8, about 13 times its first keying. Nobody had searched the hold, so
  nothing the players saw changed.
- **Weeping Beauty tea services, 1,500 gp.** Behind the Maiden's gate trap, which
  Herrick, Egil, Aren and Kai'Shem beat roped together in Session 8: 375 each,
  and Egil's Mustered retainers split 62 each. Left on the tables; credited
  anyway.
- **No XP:** the heart of the Kalla, the seeds and the pomegranate (magic), the
  gate-thing cell's silver bands (enchanted), the wreckers' crates (taken from
  people), the ship and the bone-fighters' bronze (kit).

**Pace.** Under the old rule the leaders were at about 735 of 2,000 after nine
sessions, about 80 XP a session. The deed rule on the treasure as first keyed
only got Herrick to 1,200. With the cargo raised, the four original characters
reach level 2 around Session 9 or 10, close to the one level every four sessions
the Referee wants. Holding that pace takes about 500 XP per character per
session: roughly 2,500 gp of creditable treasure a session at level 1 for five
Burners, doubling each level.

## Open for playtest

- Does XP keep pace with Traits? So far XP is the slower gate by a wide margin.
- Does monster XP run low for undead with special powers? The draugr at 35 said
  yes.
- Does "beside the hoard" need a firmer edge? Cases in between are the Referee's
  call.
