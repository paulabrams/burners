# Casting More Than One Spell

The rule stays: one spell per Cast. Sorcerers won't have the dice for more than one spell a round most of the time, so we don't need a rule for it.

## If a player asks

Let them. One Cast may hold several spells whose levels add up to no more than your Sorcerie level.

- Sorcerie 1: one 1st. Nothing changes.
- Sorcerie 2: one 2nd, or two 1sts.
- Sorcerie 3: one 3rd, a 2nd and a 1st, or three 1sts.

Each spell still burns at least its level in Fuel and taps its card from your hand. One Arcana per action, so its slots add to one of the spells, not all of them. Recasts and copies from burning count against the total.

## Why it's safe

- **The dice run out.** A level 3 Sorcerer has at most 7 Fuel. Three 1sts burn at least 3, each with a small cast total, and leave few dice to Defend with.
- **The hand runs out.** Every spell taps a card you won't have back until you burn or sleep. A round of three spells is a round you pay for later.
- **It changes nothing at Sorcerie 1.** It only matters once a Sorcerer has put a second level into Sorcerie.

## What we said no to

- One extra spell per Sorcerie level, the mirror of Veteran Attacks. Too much: a Sorcerie 1 caster would throw two spells a round.
- Levels adding up to Sorcerie + 1. Same problem at Sorcerie 1.
