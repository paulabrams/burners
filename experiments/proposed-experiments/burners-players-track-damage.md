# Players Track Damage

Monster HP and AC are out in the open, and players keep their own count.

## The rule

The Referee makes the monster's HP and AC visible to you, at least when you deal damage first, if not before.

Each time you hit, subtract the monster's AC from your damage and add what's left to your count. When the count reaches the monster's HP, it goes down.
