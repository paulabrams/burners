# Friction

Some moves are hard to pull off, so you burn one Fuel to try them, and you can't burn again. If a foe counters with more Fuel, the action fails or goes awry. The Fuel is the cost of trying: it adds nothing to a blow's damage.

**Drawing under pressure.** Burn one Fuel to draw or pick up a weapon with a foe in reach.

**Unarmed vs armed.** Burn one Fuel to make an unarmed attack against an armed opponent.

**Standing up.** Burn one Fuel to rise from the dirt with a foe pressing you in melee.

**Finding an opening.** An attack normally hits whatever is first in its line: friend, foe, or wall. Burn a 6 to shoot, strike, or cast past it: into a melee, from the second rank, around a cart or the shield wall in front of their sorcerer. Partial cover, like a low wall, does not need a 6. A shield wall can count as cover.
