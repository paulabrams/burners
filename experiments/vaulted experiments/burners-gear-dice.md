# Gear Dice

Back to the first Fuel pool, before Saxon's feedback: your gear gives you dice. It had an elegant simplicity. Saxon found real problems with it, and the fixes since have pulled the game down a longer road. This fixes the problems directly instead.

## The rule

**Your pool.** Roll your level, plus the slots of each item your Approaches cover with their Traits: the weapons and shield in your hands, and your Arcana. Sword covers weapons and shields. Sorcerie covers Arcana. Craft covers its own, like the longbow, the fighting staff, and the buckler. Heart covers the rod and the mace.

**Refresh.** Each round after the first, keep the dice you didn't use and roll your level in new dice, plus 1 for each of those items you have the Trait for. Your pool never grows past its first-round size.

**Each die belongs to its Approach.** Sword dice go on blows, Blocks, and Defends against blows. Sorcerie dice go on casts and Wards. Level dice go anywhere. This only matters if you have more than one Approach: roll each Approach's dice in its own color.

**Drop it, lose its dice.** Drop an item, stow it, or have it knocked away, and lose dice equal to its slots. It stops adding to your refresh, too.

**Pick it up, draw it.** A weapon you pick up or draw mid-fight gives no dice now. From the next round it adds 1 to your refresh if you have its Trait.

**Keeping track.** If you can't tell which dice came from the item, drop that many dice and re-roll the rest of your hand.

**Damage.** A blow with a one-handed weapon burns one die. A two-handed weapon in both hands burns a second. The second die is for a full swing or thrust with nothing in the way: not past someone in the line, not in a crawlway or a tight press, not through bars or over a wall. Hindered, it burns one. The faces are the damage. Track it with [[burners-players-track-damage]].

## At the table

- **Casters.** A Sorcerer's staff sits in her Sorcerie slots, and its dice go on her spells. A dagger in her hand gives nothing unless she has a level in Sword.
- **Shields.** Dropping the shield was a technique Chris used because it had a payoff and no rule against it. Now it costs the shield's dice, so he'll drop it less often.
- **Mixed characters.** A Veteran-Sorcerer gets both, but his sword can't feed a spell, because Sword dice can't go there. That was Saxon's "weapons help you cast" problem.

## Examples

- A level 1 Veteran, trained in arming sword (2) and heater (3), rolls 1 + 2 + 3 = 6. Each round after, she rolls 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 new dice. She drops the heater to two-hand a spear she picked up: she loses 3 dice, and if she has no *spear* Trait, her refresh falls to 1.
- A level 1 Journeyman, trained in longbow (3) under Craft, rolls 1 + 3 = 4. Each round after, he rolls 2 new dice.
- A level 1 Sorcerer with a trained staff in her Sorcerie slots (3) rolls 1 + 3 = 4. Each round after, she rolls 2 new dice.
- A level 2 Veteran-Sorcerer with a trained arming sword (2) and a trained wand (1) rolls 2 level dice, 2 Sword dice, and 1 Sorcerie die. Each round after, he rolls 2 level dice, 1 Sword die, and 1 Sorcerie die.

## Why it might work now

- **One rule.** Your Approaches say which gear counts. No choosing sides, no loss rule for casting.
- **No alpha strike.** The first pool's danger was a 10-die opening blow. Here a blow takes one die, or two with a two-hander, so a big pool buys staying power, Defends, and Friction, not one huge hit.
- **No running dry.** Saxon's refresh problem. A trained sword-and-board Veteran refreshes 3 dice a round, enough for two attacks and a Defend.
- **Traits matter.** Saxon's other point. Training is what keeps your dice coming: untrained gear gives its opening dice and nothing after.

## Watch for

- **Heart and Muster slots.** Should a Boss's Muster slots give dice, the way Sorcerie slots do? Lean: yes, for retainers in the fight, but those dice only go to his people (a retainer's attack or Defend, or Manage).
- **Craft and Heart dice.** What may they be spent on? Lean: like Sword dice when the item is a weapon.
- **Empty slots.** They give no dice now, so traveling light only matters for Fatigue and carrying loot. Is that enough?
- **Initiative.** More dice means more 3s and 4s, so heavy kit goes first more often. Is the knight in plate faster than the scout?
- **Your pack.** A worn pack fills slots and gives no dice; today, caught with it on, you roll only your level. Keep that line.
- **Big pools at the table.** 6 or more dice is a lot to read. Watch whether turns slow down.
